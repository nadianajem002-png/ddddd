🧠 Mnemonics & Memory Aids

MUDPILES = Metabolic Acidosis Causes
  • Methanol
  • Uremia
  • DKA (Diabetic Ketoacidosis)
  • Propylene glycol
  • Infection/Sepsis
  • Lactic acidosis
  • Ethylene glycol
  • Salicylates

Drug Endings:

  • -prazole = PPIs (omeprazole)
  • -tidine = H2 blockers (ranitidine)
  • -setron = 5-HT3 antiemetics (ondansetron)
  • -mab = Monoclonal antibodies (infliximab)
